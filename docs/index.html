Meet Our Team

    Bess is the founder and lead wedding planner of Bess Weddings. If it hasn't been made clear yet, hopefully her outfit makes it obvious that she simply loves love! Bess has a passion for all types of event planning and coordination. She discovered her passion in college, where she was the coordinator of numerous events for a community of kickass women, the Bruin Belles! She then began leading projects and planning events for Angel City-a nonprofit focused on adaptive sports. She can't wait to help make your wedding day as special as your love is!
Bess
Founder & Wedding Planner

You'd be hard pressed to find someone as enthusiastic and bright-eyed as him, which is why he grew up with the nickname Sunshine. With his love and appreciation for the world, Niels never leaves home without his camera as he refuses to let any magical moment go unphotographed.
